Fivestarman 45-Day Challenge

Week Six

Discussion Guide · Days 36 through 42

Guide
A word before we start

Week Six is the home stretch, and it pushes a man from reflection into motion. Days 36-42 run from facing the giant you've been avoiding, to processing an idea into a real strategy, to intentionally pursuing your wife again, to closing the gap between what you say and what you do, to being remembered for significance rather than success, to scheduling a real adventure instead of staying passive, and finally to mining the wealth God already deposited in you and putting it to work. This is the week where the challenge stops being a thought and becomes a thing you actually do.

Day 36 · Wednesday June 10

Don't Waste A Crisis

Adventurous
Summary

Day 36 reframes a crisis or giant as a disguised opportunity that scatters the timid and summons the bold. Kennedy lays out three rewards for the man willing to step onto the field of contest: wealth that comes in proportion to the problems you solve, especially the ones others ignore and especially those that throw off residual income, influence that outlasts and outweighs fame, and a legacy that reaches your descendants. Comfort is named as the real enemy of progress, the only thing separating your present from your future is the size of the challenge you're willing to face. The charge is plain: stop waiting and go solve a real problem.

Verse

There went out a champion out of the camp of the Philistines, named Goliath.

1 Samuel 17:4
Discussion Question

What is a giant, a problem, obstacle, or challenge, currently in front of you that you have been avoiding, and what would it look like to step toward it instead of away from it?

  • A problem others are ignoring that you keep stepping around, the kind that could pay residual if solved
  • The conversation you've been putting off because it's uncomfortable to confront head on
  • Naming the giant out loud, since the weak flee it and the bold are summoned by it
  • Where comfort has quietly become the reason you haven't moved on something God already flagged
  • Influence over fame, the door a solved problem opens into rooms you couldn't enter before
  • A decision today that could redirect not just your future but your kids' and theirs
  • Choosing to use what's already proven in your hand rather than waiting on the big army
Day 37 · Thursday June 11

A Strategic Idea

Entrepreneurial
Summary

Day 37 draws a sharp line between having an idea and building a strategy around it. Kennedy uses Jacob's dream as the anchor, God didn't just hand Jacob a vision, He handed him a method, the streaked and speckled flocks came with a plan attached. The lesson is that creativity without a process stays worthless, but an idea that gets curiously explored, tied to a real problem, written down, tested, protected, and networked becomes a durable source of provision. An idea is worth a dollar, a strategy is worth a million. The closing push is that financial lack is often an invitation to dream again rather than a dead end.

Verse

At the time the flocks were mating, I had a dream in which I looked up and saw that the male goats were streaked, speckled or spotted.

Genesis 31:10
Discussion Question

What is an idea you've had but never fully processed or pursued, and what has been the biggest thing holding you back from taking the next step with it?

  • The idea you keep admiring instead of processing, the dream without the strategy attached
  • A recurring frustration that's actually pointing at the problem your idea is built to solve
  • The six steps as a checklist: be curious, find the problem, write it down, test it, protect it, network it
  • Whether fear, time, or capital is the real thing stalling you, and which of those is an excuse
  • An idea written on paper versus one still floating in your head where it can't be tested
  • Bringing one trusted person in to pressure-test it rather than guarding it alone
  • Treating a lean season as a prompt to dream again, not proof the idea won't work
Day 38 · Friday June 12

Intentionally Date Your Wife

Gallant
Summary

Day 38 calls a married man to keep courting his wife rather than coasting on the fact that he already won her. The heart of it is intentionality, a real date is planned ahead, not a last minute where do you want to go, and it pulls her out of the daily grind in a way that says she's still worth pursuing. Kennedy's list of date tips all point the same direction: dress up, make reservations, spend like it matters, line up childcare, send something ahead, open doors, and stay focused on her instead of drifting into talk about kids, work, or problems. The challenge is simple and concrete, make a date with your wife.

Verse

All night long on my bed I looked for the one my heart loves; I looked for him but did not find him.

Song of Solomon 3:1
Discussion Question

When was the last time you planned a truly intentional, thought-out date for your wife, and what is one thing you could do to raise the bar the next time you take her out?

  • The difference between proximity and pursuit, being around her a lot is not the same as pursuing her
  • Planning the night yourself instead of handing her the decision with where do you want to go
  • One element that's for her specifically, something that proves you thought about her, not just convenience
  • The hard rule for the evening: no kids talk, no work talk, fully present
  • Lining up the babysitter and locking the date well in advance so it actually happens
  • Investing real money or effort as a way of marking that she's worth it, especially in a lean stretch
  • Returning to a place that mattered to you both on purpose, after letting it slide
Day 39 · Saturday June 13

Do What You Say

Faithful
Summary

Day 39 presses on authentic manhood as the closing of the gap between word and deed. The Bible speaks of clouds without rain, men who are always promising but never delivering, and Kennedy's point is that affirmation without follow-through is delusion. You set yourself apart from mediocrity the moment you decide to follow through and finish well, and you'll never gain real momentum until you become a doer of your words. The same applies to your walk with God, which deserves your foremost effort rather than a casual stroll. The day closes with the 12 Keys to Being an Authentic Man, concrete handles on what that integrity looks like across the practical areas of a man's life.

Verse

Show me your faith without deeds, and I will show you my faith by what I do.

James 2:18
Discussion Question

Think of a promise or commitment you've made, to God, your wife, your kids, or yourself, that you haven't fully followed through on. What would it take to close that gap this week?

  • The cloud without rain, where you're affirming something out loud that your follow-through isn't backing up
  • A promise to your wife or kids that's been quietly carried over week after week
  • A commitment to yourself, fitness, reading, prayer, that keeps restarting instead of sticking
  • Whether your walk with God gets your foremost effort or just a casual stroll
  • The 12 Keys as a self-audit: order, integrity, discipline, prudence, wisdom, the rest
  • Naming the one gap and the single next action that would close it inside seven days
  • The reminder that you redefine yourself in a moment but prove it over a lifetime
Day 40 · Sunday June 14

Be Remembered

Philanthropic
Summary

Day 40 confronts the audit every man eventually runs on his own life, did any of it matter, will I be remembered. Kennedy's answer is that significance, not success, is the measure, and significance is only found in what's eternal, which means people. Stuff rusts, gets stolen, and gets forgotten, but what lasts is what you pour into the living, those who will carry your story forward. The day offers keys to eternal giving, start small, give regularly, vet the charity, invest in people over buildings, keep a relationship with the recipient, and don't do it just for the tax benefit. The challenge is simple and habit-building, give something to someone today.

Verse

Store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where moth and rust do not destroy, and where thieves do not break in and steal.

Matthew 6:20
Discussion Question

When your life is over, what do you want the people who knew you best to say you stood for, and does the way you're currently living actually reflect that?

  • The gap between what you'd want said at your funeral and how this week actually looked
  • Significance over success, measuring a life by what it pours into people rather than what it accumulates
  • Where your time and money currently flow, and whether that aim matches what you say you stand for
  • Investing in the living: a person you could mentor, encourage, or simply show up for
  • A turned-down comfort or paycheck for the sake of the calling, and what that revealed
  • Starting a small, regular habit of giving rather than waiting for a grand gesture later
  • The people who will carry your story, and what you're actively depositing in them now
Day 41 · Monday June 15

The Field Of Contest Calls You!

Adventurous
Summary

Day 41 reframes adventure not as a hobby but as part of how men are made, we're sharpened through clash and challenge, not comfort. Iron sharpens iron, and a man is shaped not by the caress but by the contest. The field calls a man to dig deep, fight through fear, exercise aching muscles, and risk getting hurt for the sake of growth. The companion reading sharpens it into a diagnosis of passivity, the quiet killer of a man's motivation, leadership, and relationships, and points to Jonathan, who scaled a cliff with only his armor-bearer and stirred a whole paralyzed army to join the fight. The challenge is simple and concrete, stop talking about it and actually schedule a real adventure.

Verse

As iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another.

Proverbs 27:17
Discussion Question

What is one challenge or risk you have been too comfortable to pursue, and what would it reveal about you if you actually showed up for it?

  • The adventure you keep saying someday about instead of putting an actual date on the calendar
  • Passivity as the real enemy, retreating or surrendering rather than confronting the battle
  • Where comfort has dulled your motivation, made decisions harder, or softened your leadership at home
  • The Jonathan move, taking the risk yourself and stirring others out of their paralysis with you
  • A challenge that would sharpen you through the clash, since men aren't sharpened by ease
  • What showing up would reveal, courage you've been doubting, or a fear you've been managing around
  • One concrete antidote: set a goal and act, make the decision, stand up even when it's hard
Day 42 · Tuesday June 16

Make Some Money!

Entrepreneurial
Summary

Day 42 reframes wealth as something already deposited in you by God rather than something to chase out in the world. You don't have to drift from job to job looking for income, you can dig deep and draw on your God-given gifts to meet your needs, satisfy your wants, and fund your purpose. The call to action is blunt, discover the gift, cultivate it, sharpen the skill set, and others will flock to pour money into your lap for it. The companion reading dismantles the idea that poverty is holy and replaces it with a theology of increase, using the rich young ruler and the Parable of the Talents to warn against the maintainer, the man who merely holds ground out of fear instead of multiplying what he was given.

Verse

But remember the Lord your God, for it is he who gives you the ability to produce wealth.

Deuteronomy 8:18
Discussion Question

What God-given gift or skill do you have that you've been underutilizing, and what would it look like to begin building something with it?

  • The reservoir already in you, a gift to mine rather than an income to go hunt for elsewhere
  • The maintainer warning, doing just enough to hold ground while blaming circumstances for the lack
  • A skill people already pay for or ask you about that you've never built into something real
  • The hindrance that actually stops you: creativity, capital, business acumen, or time
  • A side hustle or first concrete step that turns the gift into provision and funds your purpose
  • Multiplying what you were given versus burying it out of fear, the lesson of the talents
  • Sharpening the gift so the work gets good enough that others come looking for it

How can we pray for you this week?
